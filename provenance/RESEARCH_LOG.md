# Research log

## 2026-08-19 — Initial blueprint research

Question: Which mechanics and interaction qualities made the historical title memorable, and how can they be translated into an original current browser game?

Public findings abstracted into this blueprint:

- text/command-driven normal play rather than continuous field navigation;
- exploration as the encounter trigger;
- turn combat with short queued command planning;
- low-bandwidth still-image presentation;
- asynchronous shared world and free-form community;
- crafting, auctions, collection books, and build/type systems;
- approachable repetition needing stronger tactical variety.

Production translation:

- semantic numbered command deck;
- original route graph and deterministic encounter service;
- original combat/loot/card systems;
- async traces/BBS/guilds before trade;
- clean-room terminology and assets;
- modern accessibility, security, moderation, and cost controls.

No source assets, code, data dumps, formulas, maps, dialogue, or UI reconstruction were imported.
