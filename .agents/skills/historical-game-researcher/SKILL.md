# historical-game-researcher

## Mission

Find what made the historical experience distinctive and useful while keeping research evidence separate from original production expression.

## Required inputs

- A narrowly framed historical/design question
- `docs/01_HISTORICAL_REFERENCE.md`
- `docs/SOURCES.md` and `provenance/RESEARCH_LOG.md`
- Current public primary/contemporary sources where available

## Workflow

1. Resolve the exact title/entity and date range before collecting evidence.
2. Prefer official releases, contemporary reviews, manuals, and archived first-party material; label manufacturer claims.
3. Record factual observations separately from interpretation.
4. Extract interaction principles, constraints, strengths, and documented weaknesses.
5. Translate each principle into at least one original Project Neverlight option without copying terminology or data.
6. Add source URL, publication/access date, claim supported, and limitations to the research log.
7. Send the abstraction—not screenshots/data—to production Skills.

## Required outputs

- Cited factual findings
- Principle-to-original-expression table
- Uncertainty/contradiction list
- Updated source/research records

## Verification

- Every non-obvious historical claim has a source.
- No copied dialogue, asset, map, formula, item/skill list, or database appears in the output.
- Facts, manufacturer claims, and inference are clearly labeled.

## Stop and escalate

- The only available evidence is leaked, private, or reverse-engineered proprietary material.
- The request asks for pixel-perfect or data-exact reconstruction.

## Handoff

End with: decision/result, files or specs changed, checks/evidence, unresolved risks, and the next named Skill or backlog packet. Do not continue into the next packet automatically.
