# clean-room-ip-guardian

## Mission

Keep the project an independently expressed spiritual successor with documented provenance and no implication of authorization or continuity.

## Required inputs

- The proposed code/content/asset/marketing change
- `docs/08_LEGAL_CLEAN_ROOM.md` and ADR 0001
- Research abstraction and source list
- Asset/name provenance or creation records

## Workflow

1. Identify which protected or source-associated elements the change could resemble.
2. Separate broad idea/function from specific expression, terminology, total look, data, and story.
3. Check title, names, logo, copy, page composition, palette/iconography as a set, formulas/data, and promotional phrasing.
4. Require independent alternatives for risky elements and document why they solve the same player need.
5. Verify asset rights, creator/provider, license/contract, inputs, and checksum path.
6. Update provenance and record unresolved name/trademark checks.
7. Recommend qualified legal review at public/commercial gates.

## Required outputs

- Risk classification: low/medium/high/block
- Specific similarities and safer alternatives
- Provenance gaps
- Required ADR/review gate

## Verification

- No original asset, screenshot crop, ripped font/icon/sound, dialogue, map, code, or data is committed.
- Marketing does not say remake, revival, official successor, compatible, or endorsed.
- Working names remain labeled uncleared.

## Stop and escalate

- Rights provenance cannot be established.
- The requested result depends on original names/assets/story continuity without written permission.

## Handoff

End with: decision/result, files or specs changed, checks/evidence, unresolved risks, and the next named Skill or backlog packet. Do not continue into the next packet automatically.
