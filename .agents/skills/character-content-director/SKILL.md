# character-content-director

## Mission

Create memorable adult women and supporting cast whose jobs, motives, contradictions, and gameplay roles matter more than collectible appearance.

## Required inputs

- `docs/02_PRODUCT_VISION.md` and `docs/07_CONTENT_R15_SAFETY.md`
- Character/content purpose in the game loop
- World glossary and existing relationships
- Asset provenance/content-review requirements

## Workflow

1. Define canonical age 20+, occupation, agency, goal, fear, contradiction, relationships, and gameplay function.
2. Create an original silhouette/visual brief with unmistakable adult coding and no copied artist/game design.
3. Write the general-audience version first.
4. If requested, write a separate optional non-explicit suggestive brief with identical mechanics and a general fallback.
5. Review dialogue for consent, decline paths, stereotype/reward-object problems, age ambiguity, and explicitness.
6. Add alt-text intent, prohibited motifs, provenance requirements, and reviewer checklist.
7. Integrate character appearances into quests/systems rather than isolated fanservice rewards.

## Required outputs

- Character sheet
- General visual/dialogue brief
- Optional suggestive brief and fallback mapping
- Relationship/system hooks
- Content safety and provenance checklist

## Verification

- Every named depicted humanoid has age >=20.
- Declining flirtation has no mechanical punishment.
- No explicit nudity/acts, coercion, age ambiguity, or sexual-content monetization.
- Character has non-romantic agency and gameplay relevance.

## Stop and escalate

- A requested depiction is minor/ambiguous, explicit, coercive, or lacks a general fallback.
- The brief asks to imitate a living artist or protected character.

## Handoff

End with: decision/result, files or specs changed, checks/evidence, unresolved risks, and the next named Skill or backlog packet. Do not continue into the next packet automatically.
