# First region adult cast — general-audience sheets

These are original working designs for the internal Alpha bundle. Every named depicted
character is documented as an adult, and every optional suggestive reference is disabled.
The general-audience brief is the complete production fallback; no mechanic, reward, or
dialogue branch depends on a suggestive presentation.

## Mireia Voss — 27

- Occupation: relic appraiser and provenance clerk.
- Goal: make the city's recovered objects legible without turning uncertainty into a sale.
- Fear: that a confident label will erase the person who found an object.
- Contradiction: she distrusts superstition but keeps a private notebook of improbable coincidences.
- Relationships: professional respect with Noa; friendly friction with Luca; clear boundaries with Safi.
- Gameplay function: explains item source, affix range, bind state, and codex context.
- General visual brief: adult woman with an upright posture, cropped dark hair, a weatherproof
  blue coat, brass measuring tools, a square notebook, and a practical satchel. Her silhouette
  reads as a desk-and-field researcher rather than a collectible pose.
- Accessibility description: “A dark-haired adult appraiser in a blue coat holds a notebook and
  a brass caliper beside a rain-marked worktable.”
- Dialogue rule: a player may decline a conversation or appraisal prompt without losing a route,
  reward, or codex entry.
- Prohibited motifs: reward stripping, sexualized framing, age ambiguity, or romance as payment.
- Provenance: `asset.char.mireia-voss.general`; original written brief and abstract placeholder;
  no final image is approved.

## Luca Aster — 24

- Occupation: bounty and dispatch courier.
- Goal: prove that a safe route can be faster than a reckless shortcut.
- Fear: arriving with a message after the decision window has closed.
- Contradiction: he improvises constantly while insisting every route needs a written fallback.
- Relationships: competitive friendship with Mireia; trusts Safi's boundaries; asks Noa for repairs.
- Gameplay function: presents dispatches, route risk, session length, and retreat expectations.
- General visual brief: adult man with a lean runner's build, visible weather marks on a short
  amber jacket, a sealed document tube, reinforced boots, and a route slate. The pose shows
  movement stopping to read, not a heroic combat display.
- Accessibility description: “An adult courier in a short amber rain jacket stands with a sealed
  document tube and points at a route slate.”
- Dialogue rule: a player can reject a risky dispatch or choose the longer route with no penalty
  beyond the stated time/risk tradeoff.
- Prohibited motifs: coercion, humiliation, age-coded school styling, or flirtation tied to access.
- Provenance: `asset.char.luca-aster.general`; original written brief and abstract placeholder;
  no final image is approved.

## Safi Ren — 31

- Occupation: guild broker and asynchronous notice editor.
- Goal: make shared discoveries useful without exposing players to unwanted contact.
- Fear: that a helpful notice will become a demand that someone cannot safely refuse.
- Contradiction: she is warm in person but edits every public sentence three times.
- Relationships: mediates Luca and Mireia; hires Noa for accessibility checks; maintains a quiet
  professional network at the quay.
- Gameplay function: grants dispatches, explains safe resume/retry behavior, and introduces codex
  sharing without direct social pressure.
- General visual brief: adult woman with a broad-shouldered practical silhouette, a rust-red
  waterproof wrap, a folding notice board, ink-stained gloves, and a clearly visible boundary
  token on her lanyard. The design emphasizes an organizer at work.
- Accessibility description: “An adult guild broker in a rust-red waterproof wrap holds a folding
  notice board with large readable cards.”
- Dialogue rule: decline, mute, and block choices remain available and never reduce rewards or
  progression.
- Prohibited motifs: romance gate, coercive networking, sexualized authority, or ambiguous age.
- Provenance: `asset.char.safi-ren.general`; original written brief and abstract placeholder;
  no final image is approved.

## Noa Quill — 29

- Occupation: prosthetics and field-equipment engineer.
- Goal: give every Pathfinder time to compare an item before committing to it.
- Fear: that a rushed repair will turn a small inconvenience into a permanent limitation.
- Contradiction: her humor is dry, but her work is intensely patient and protective.
- Relationships: collaborates with Mireia on provenance; maintains Luca's boots; audits Safi's
  notice readability.
- Gameplay function: explains equipment comparison, derived stats, repair language, and the
  three build paths: tempo, ward, and signal-reading.
- General visual brief: adult woman with a sturdy mechanic's silhouette, a charcoal work apron,
  bright tool tabs, a removable forearm brace used as a work fixture, and a magnifying visor
  resting above her brow. The brace is equipment, not a joke or a damage prop.
- Accessibility description: “An adult engineer in a charcoal apron adjusts a tool fixture beside
  a clearly labeled equipment comparison board.”
- Dialogue rule: choosing “I need more time” keeps the comparison open and has no hidden cost.
- Prohibited motifs: body-based jokes, pity framing, forced intimacy, or sexualized injury imagery.
- Provenance: `asset.char.noa-quill.general`; original written brief and abstract placeholder;
  no final image is approved.
