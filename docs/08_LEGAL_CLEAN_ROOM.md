# 08 — Legal and clean-room production plan

> This is a risk-control design document, not legal advice. Obtain qualified review before public launch or commercial use.

## 1. Product posture

Project Neverlight is an independent original game inspired by broad, unprotectable ideas and publicly described design principles. It must not be marketed as a remake, revival, private server, sequel, compatibility layer, or official successor.

## 2. Clean-room workflow

### Research side

- Records public factual descriptions, dates, genre conventions, and high-level interaction principles.
- Produces abstract requirement statements.
- Does not import original assets, data dumps, code, formulas, maps, or extensive screenshots.
- Stores citations in `docs/SOURCES.md` and notes in `provenance/RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

### Production side

- Works from approved abstract specs in this repository.
- Creates independent terminology, story, characters, art direction, code, data, and balancing.
- Does not browse or ingest source-game assets while implementing.
- Documents original source/provenance of every asset.

For a small team, the separation is procedural rather than personnel-based: research notes must be translated into abstractions, then implementation references only the abstraction.

## 3. Red-line comparison test

Before public alpha, compare the project and inspiration at a high level for confusing similarity in:

- title and logo;
- world/faction/character terminology;
- page composition and navigation sequence;
- iconography, frames, palettes, and typography as a total look;
- named systems, item/skill lists, formulas, maps, and story beats;
- promotional language.

Similarity in “static image + text + command links” alone is not the design target; the total expression must remain original.

## 4. Asset rules

Allowed with documentation:

- commissioned original art with written commercial rights;
- self-created art;
- properly licensed fonts, icons, textures, and sound if sound is later added;
- generated images only when the provider/model/license and training/use constraints are acceptable, with human review and provenance.

Forbidden:

- source screenshots or crops;
- tracing source art or a living artist’s distinctive style on request;
- ripped fonts/sounds/icons;
- “temporary” copyrighted placeholders committed to the repository;
- assets with unknown license origin.

## 5. Name clearance

“Project Neverlight,” “Vesper Ark,” “Lattice,” and all character names are working terms. Before public announcement:

- search game/store/domain/social uses;
- search relevant trademarks in target regions;
- assess phonetic and visual similarity;
- reserve domain/social handles only after approval;
- replace uncleared names in one coordinated content migration.

## 6. Reference claims

Do not quote manufacturer population/item-count claims as project promises. Research documents may label them as historical manufacturer claims. Marketing should describe only Project Neverlight’s own tested features.

## 7. Public documentation

Before public access, create:

- Terms of Service;
- Privacy Notice and cookie/storage disclosure;
- Community Guidelines and RMT policy;
- IP/takedown contact;
- content and account eligibility statement;
- asset credits and open-source notices;
- refund/support policy if commerce is enabled.

## 8. Decision gate

Any plan to use original names, assets, screenshots, or direct story continuity requires explicit rights-holder permission documented in the repository. Without that permission, the clean-room rule remains absolute.
