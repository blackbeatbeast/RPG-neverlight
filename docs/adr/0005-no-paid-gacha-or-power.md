# ADR 0005 — No paid gacha or paid power

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-19

## Decision
Random rare items/cards are earned through play only. Money cannot buy combat stats, drop rate, stamina, market currency, or tradeable value. Future support may buy direct non-tradeable cosmetics or recognition.

## Consequences
Monetization options narrow but player trust and economy integrity improve. Entitlements must remain outside authoritative progression packages.
