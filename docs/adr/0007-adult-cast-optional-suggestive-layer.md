# ADR 0007 — Adult cast and optional suggestive layer

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-19

## Decision
Every named depicted humanoid is 20+. General-audience presentation is complete/default. Optional non-explicit suggestive assets may be enabled only after review and always have equivalent general fallbacks. Mechanics never differ.

## Consequences
Content metadata, provenance, settings, and asset selection must encode the distinction. Public/ad surfaces use general assets. There is no explicit-content tier.
