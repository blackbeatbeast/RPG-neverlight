# Sources and research log

Accessed 2026-08-19 unless stated otherwise. Sources inform factual history, tooling setup, cost assumptions, and risk controls. They do not grant permission to copy protected expression.

## Historical game

1. GAME Watch, “モバイルゲームレビュー『ネバーワールドオンライン』” (2008-07-09)
   https://game.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/20080709/nw.htm
   Supports: platforms/pricing at the time, text/command interface, no normal field map, exploration encounters, turn combat, three-turn queue, community/crafting/auction/collection descriptions, and contemporary critique.

2. 4Gamer / Ambition release, “オンラインRPG『ネバーワールドオンライン』がサービス提供10年めに突入” (2013-07-22)
   https://www.4gamer.net/games/226/G022639/20130722056/
   Supports: 2004 launch anniversary context and manufacturer description of free-word conversation/free play style. Manufacturer claims must be labeled as such.

3. Ambition archived press page, private dungeon/type/skill feature release
   https://www.ambition.ne.jp/press.php?id=50
   Supports broad evidence that types/classes, proficiencies, skills, and private dungeons existed. Do not copy their names/data.

4. Majingai reference site
   https://majingai.com/
   User-provided visual/interaction reference only. Do not copy layout, assets, CSS, text, or total look.

## Copyright and content/advertising risk

12. Agency for Cultural Affairs, Japan, copyright guidance
    https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/chosakuken/taisetsu/point/index.html
    Supports the general rule that game images/music/characters are protected and use depends on rights-holder permission/guidelines.

13. Google Publisher Policies, sexually explicit content
    https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10502938?hl=en
    Supports the prohibition on explicit sexual content and underage/non-consensual sexual themes.

14. Google Publisher Restrictions, sexual content
    https://support.google.com/publisherpolicies/answer/10400657?hl=en
    Supports the need to recheck whether suggestive content restricts ad serving and to keep general-audience placements. Policies may change.

## Research limitations

- Service history pages and manufacturer releases are incomplete archives.
- Historical descriptions are not a complete specification and may contain promotional claims.
- This blueprint intentionally avoids reconstructing exact formulas, databases, maps, or asset sets.
- Legal, trademark, privacy, rating, and ad-network conclusions require current specialist review before launch.
